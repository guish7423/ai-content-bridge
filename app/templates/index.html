FAQ

Everything you need to know

How is this different from Google Translate / ChatGPT? ▼
Google Translate does literal word-for-word translation. ChatGPT gives you a generic translation. We do cultural transcreation — we analyze your content, understand the context, and rewrite it as if a native English marketing expert wrote it. Idioms, humor, cultural references — all reworked for each platform.
Is my content private? ▼
Yes. Your content is processed in real-time and not stored on our servers. We use encrypted API connections to LLM providers. We never train on your data. Your business content stays yours.
Do I need my own API key? ▼
Free tier works in demo mode (simulated translations) so you can test the UX. Starter and Pro plans include API access — no additional key needed. Enterprise customers can bring their own key.
Which platforms do you support? ▼
Currently: X/Twitter (with thread support), LinkedIn (articles & posts), and Reddit. We're adding Substack, Medium, and WordPress blog support soon.
Can I publish directly from the platform? ▼
Yes! Starter and Pro plans include direct publishing to X/Twitter. More platforms coming. Free plan lets you copy the output manually.
